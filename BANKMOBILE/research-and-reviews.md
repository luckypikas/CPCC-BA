Government Regulations & Criteria

To handle student financial aid distributions (Title IV funds), companies like BankMobile must comply strictly with the U.S. Department of Education's Cash Management Regulations (34 CFR Part 668): [1] (https://www.cpcc.edu/admissions-financial-aid/paying-college/tuition-refund-and-tax-policies)
• Consent: They cannot force students to open a proprietary bank account.
• Transparency: They must explicitly disclose all contract details, revenue-sharing models, and fee schedules publicly on the college's website. [1] (https://www.cpcc.edu/admissions-financial-aid/paying-college/tuition-refund-and-tax-policies)
• No Mitigation of Choice: Depositing refunds to a student’s pre-existing, third-party bank account must be as timely and accessible as using the vendor's branded card. [1] (https://catalog.cpcc.edu/student-services/financial-aid/processes-federal-regulations/), [2] (https://www.cpcc.edu/admissions-financial-aid/paying-college/tuition-refund-and-tax-policies)

Where to File Complaints & Escalate


1. Official Financial & Education Regulators

• Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB): Submit an official complaint directly via the CFPB Complaint Portal. The CFPB enforces federal consumer financial laws and forces banks to respond to complaints within 15 days.
• U.S. Department of Education Federal Student Aid (FSA) Ombudsman: File a formal feedback report through the FSA Feedback Center regarding third-party cash management compliance.
• Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC): BankMobile is a financial technology platform, not a bank. Its partner bank is First Carolina Bank. You can file a banking complaint via the FDIC Information and Support Center. [1] (https://www.firstcarolinabank.com/bmtechnologies/contact), [2] (https://money.usnews.com/loans/student-loans/reviews/nelnet-bank-student), [3] (https://www.cpcc.edu/admissions-financial-aid/paying-college/tuition-refund-and-tax-policies)

2. Consumer & Student Associations

• Better Business Bureau (BBB): File a public consumer case on the BM Technologies BBB Profile.
• Student Borrower Protection Center (SBPC): Report predatory banking behavior or disbursement issues to the SBPC Action Platform. [1] (https://www.bbb.org/us/ne/lincoln/profile/student-loan-services/nelnet-national-educational-loan-network-0714-209000034/complaints)
