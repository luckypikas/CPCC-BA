Customer reviews and complaints for BankMobile (BM Technologies, Inc.) are predominantly negative across major consumer platforms, with a TrustScore of 2.5/5 on Trustpilot and a 2.1-star rating on PissedConsumer. As a digital banking platform serving roughly one-third of U.S. college students receiving financial aid refunds, most complaints center around locked accounts, delayed student disbursements, and prolonged customer service wait times. [1] (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.bankmobile.com), [2] (https://bm-technologies.pissedconsumer.com/review.html), [3] (https://www.bbb.org/us/nc/rocky-mount/profile/banking-services/bm-technologies-inc-0593-90365609/complaints)

Common Complaints and Pain Points

• Account Locks and Verification Issues: Users frequently report unexpected account lockouts that last for months, often triggered by updating refund preferences, linking external bank accounts, or attempting transfers. A consensus on Reddit highlights that identity verification requirements are repetitive and difficult to resolve through the BankMobile App. [1] (https://www.reddit.com/r/financialaid/comments/1n2tp8m/bank_mobile_is_horrific/), [2] (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.bankmobile.com)
• Delayed Refunds and Fund Access: Because BankMobile acts as a major disbursement partner for over 700 colleges, students routinely complain about delayed financial aid refunds, declined transactions on virtual or physical debit cards, and blocked transfers to third-party apps like Cash App. [1] (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bankmobile/id578435033?see-all=reviews), [2] (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.bankmobile.com)
• Poor Customer Service: Across the Better Business Bureau (BBB) and consumer review sites, customers cite phone hold times exceeding 1 to 2 hours, dropped calls, unhelpful support staff, and fraud dispute processes that can take up to 180 days. [1] (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.bankmobile.com), [2] (https://bm-technologies.pissedconsumer.com/review.html), [3] (https://www.bbb.org/us/nc/rocky-mount/profile/banking-services/bm-technologies-inc-0593-90365609/complaints)
• App and Technical Glitches: Reviewers on the Apple App Store report that in-app money transfers, card controls, and mobile check functionalities frequently fail or result in repeated error messages. [1] (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bankmobile/id578435033?see-all=reviews)

Positive Feedback and Workarounds

• Legitimate Infrastructure: Some reviewers and students acknowledge that the underlying setup—partnered with established institutions like First Carolina Bank and Customers Bank—functions securely for standard, uninterrupted student aid disbursements. [1] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXqmWIvVhik), [2] (https://bm-technologies.pissedconsumer.com/review.html)
• Desktop Browser Workaround: Some users note that bypassing the mobile app and accessing their account via a desktop browser (such as Chrome) can occasionally prevent or resolve certain login and verification glitches. [1] (https://www.reddit.com/r/financialaid/comments/1n2tp8m/bank_mobile_is_horrific/)
• Rare Support Successes: A small fraction of reviews mention positive experiences when reaching alternative messaging or resolution teams that manage to clear up address or account snags promptly. [1] (https://www.firstcarolinabank.com/bankmobile/home), [2] (https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.bankmobile.com)
