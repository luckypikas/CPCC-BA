GROUP 1: THE GEOMETRY & MATH CODES (GRID BASICS)

1. Horizontal Axis
• Synonyms: X-Axis / The Floor / The Bottom Line
• Definition: The flat boundary line running left-to-right at the bottom of your chart.
2. Vertical Axis
• Synonyms: Y-Axis / The Wall / The Upright Line
• Definition: The straight standing boundary line running bottom-to-top on the left side.
3. Origin
• Synonyms: The Zero Point / Center-Zero / Starting Point
• Definition: The absolute bottom-left corner where both axes cross; its value is always (0,0).
4. Tick Marks
• Synonyms: Grid Lines / Scale Marks / Interval Lines
• Definition: The small evenly-spaced hash lines on the axes showing numbers (like 10, 20, 30).
5. Coordinate
• Synonyms: Ordered Pair / The Point / Data Point / (X, Y)
• Definition: An exact address on your grid made of a horizontal position and a vertical position.
6. Rise
• Synonyms: Vertical Change / ΔY / Height Change
• Definition: How many spaces a line moves straight up or straight down between two points.
7. Run
• Synonyms: Horizontal Change / ΔX / Width Change
• Definition: How many spaces a line moves flatly left or right between two points.
8. Slope
• Synonyms: Steepness / Rate of Change / Pitch / Gradient
• Definition: Calculated as Rise divided by Run. It measures how aggressively a curve tilts.
9. Intersect
• Synonyms: Cross / Overlap / Meet / Intersection Point
• Definition: The exact spot where two distinct curves smash into each other on the grid.
10. Intercept
• Synonyms: Axis Hit / Wall Touch / Floor Touch
• Definition: The location where a curve runs directly into the flat horizontal floor or vertical wall.

GROUP 2: THE MOVEMENT & SHIFT CODES (DYNAMICS)

11. Shift
• Synonyms: Total Migration / Curve Jump / New Position
• Definition: When an outside force causes an entire line to physically un-anchor and move to a new spot.
12. Movement Along
• Synonyms: Slide / Glide / Point Migration
• Definition: When you do not move the line; you just slide your finger from one tick mark to another on the same line.
13. Rightward Shift
• Synonyms: Expansion / Increase / Growth / Outward Shift
• Definition: Moving a curve away from the vertical wall. Remember: More = Right.
14. Leftward Shift
• Synonyms: Contraction / Decrease / Reduction / Inward Shift
• Definition: Moving a curve closer to the vertical wall. Remember: Less = Left.
15. Constant
• Synonyms: Fixed / Unchanging / Static / Baseline / Given
• Definition: A number or condition that stays exactly the same throughout the entire problem puzzle.
16. Variable
• Synonyms: The Unknown / Floating Input / Factor / x or y
• Definition: An element on your graph or math word problem that changes value based on conditions.
17. Inverse Relationship
• Synonyms: Negative Slope / Indirect / Opposite Movement
• Definition: When one variable climbs up, the other variable automatically sinks down.
18. Direct Relationship
• Synonyms: Positive Slope / Identical Movement / Partners
• Definition: When both variables move up together or drop down together in perfect harmony.
19. Linear
• Synonyms: Straight-Line / Non-Curved / Uniform
• Definition: A relationship that forms a perfectly flat straight line on your graph grid.
20. Non-Linear
• Synonyms: Curved / Exponential / Arc
• Definition: A relationship where the line bends, waves, or bows out on the graph canvas.

GROUP 3: THE ECONOMICS CORE CODES (DEMAND & SUPPLY)

21. Demand Curve
• Synonyms: Buyer's Line / Marginal Benefit (MB) / Willingness to Pay
• Definition: The downward-sloping line showing how much product consumers want at different prices.
22. Supply Curve
• Synonyms: Seller's Line / Marginal Cost (MC) / Producer's Line
• Definition: The upward-sloping line showing how many items factories want to sell at different prices.
23. Quantity Demanded (\(Q_{D}\))
• Synonyms: Amount Desired / Buyer Purchases / Consumption Vol
• Definition: The specific number of items buyers will purchase at one single exact price mark.
24. Quantity Supplied (\(Q_{S}\))
• Synonyms: Amount Produced / Factory Output / Inventory Vol
• Definition: The specific number of items factories will manufacture at one single exact price mark.
25. Equilibrium
• Synonyms: Market Clearing / Sweet Spot / Balanced State / Stabilized Point
• Definition: The perfect intersection where Quantity Supplied matches Quantity Demanded exactly.
26. Shortage
• Synonyms: Scarcity / Excess Demand / Under-supply / Deficit
• Definition: When the price is too low, causing buyers to want more items than sellers brought to market.
27. Surplus
• Synonyms: Glut / Excess Supply / Over-stock / Leftovers
• Definition: When the price is set too high, causing items to sit unsold on retail shelves.
28. Elasticity
• Synonyms: Sensitivity / Responsiveness / Flexibility
• Definition: How violently consumers react or change their behavior when a price changes.
29. Elastic
• Synonyms: Highly Sensitive / Stretchy / Price-Reactive
• Definition: A flat curve showing that consumers will completely boycott an item if the price goes up even a tiny bit.
30. Inelastic
• Synonyms: Unresponsive / Rigid / Stubborn / Non-Reactive
• Definition: A steep curve showing that consumers must buy an item (like medicine or gas) no matter how expensive it gets.

GROUP 4: RETAIL, BUSINESS, & COST CODES

31. Revenue
• Synonyms: Gross Receipts / Total Sales / Top-Line Income
• Definition: The total raw money a business pulls in before paying for any expenses. (Price \(\times \) Quantity).
32. Profit
• Synonyms: Net Income / Bottom Line / Earnings / Surplus Value
• Definition: The actual cash left over for the business owner after subtracting all costs from revenue.
33. Fixed Costs
• Synonyms: Overhead / Sunk Costs / Baseline Expenses / Rent
• Definition: Bills that must be paid regardless of whether you produce zero items or a million items.
34. Variable Costs
• Synonyms: Operating Costs / Input Costs / Production Expenses
• Definition: Bills that fluctuate directly based on how much inventory you manufacture (like raw materials).
35. Marginal
• Synonyms: Incremental / Additional / The Next Unit / Extra
• Definition: The cost or benefit of doing exactly one more unit of action.
36. Consumer
• Synonyms: Buyer / End User / Customer / Household
• Definition: The individual playing the game who spends cash to purchase items.
37. Producer
• Synonyms: Firm / Business / Seller / Manufacturer / Vendor
• Definition: The individual playing the game who builds items to make a profit.
38. Equilibrium Price (\(P^{*}\))
• Synonyms: Market Price / Target Price / Clearing Price
• Definition: The exact dollar amount where an item sells without creating a shortage or a surplus.
39. Equilibrium Quantity (\(Q^{*}\))
• Synonyms: Target Volume / Clearing Quantity / Optimized Output
• Definition: The exact number of units moving through the market at the equilibrium price.
40. Market
• Synonyms: Trading Arena / Exchange Hub / Industry
• Definition: Any digital or physical space where buyers and sellers swap cash for goods.

GROUP 5: ADVANCED WELFARE & GOVERNMENT INTERVENTION CODES

41. Consumer Surplus
• Synonyms: Buyer's Bonus / Unearned Savings / Deal Value
• Definition: The mental or financial savings a buyer gets when they would have paid $50, but got it for $30.
42. Producer Surplus
• Synonyms: Seller's Bonus / Extra Profit / Vendor Premium
• Definition: The extra money a seller makes when they were willing to sell for $10, but the market paid them $30.
43. Deadweight Loss (DWL)
• Synonyms: Economic Waste / Lost Welfare / Inefficiency Triangle
• Definition: The value of trades that should have happened but were blocked by an outside tax or regulation.
44. Price Ceiling
• Synonyms: Maximum Cap / Upper Legal Limit / Rent Control
• Definition: A government rule blocking prices from rising any higher. (Must sit below equilibrium).
45. Price Floor
• Synonyms: Minimum Support / Lower Legal Limit / Price Prop
• Definition: A government rule blocking prices from dropping any lower. (Must sit above equilibrium).
46. Tax Incidence
• Synonyms: Tax Burden / Cost Split / Tax Payment Distribution
• Definition: The study of who actually loses the most money when a new tax is passed (buyers vs. sellers).
47. Subsidy
• Synonyms: Financial Grant / Government Incentive / Bonus Payout
• Definition: The opposite of a tax; the government pays you money to produce or buy an item.
48. Welfare
• Synonyms: Total Surplus / Societal Benefit / Economic Health
• Definition: The combined value of Consumer Surplus and Producer Surplus added together.
49. Externality
• Synonyms: Side Effect / Spillover Effect / Third-Party Impact
• Definition: An unintended cost or benefit hitting an innocent bystander who didn't buy the product.
50. Negative Externality
• Synonyms: External Cost / Spillover Harm / Pollution Damage
• Definition: When a factory creates an item but dumps toxic smoke into a neighborhood nearby.
