PART 2: THE EXTREME LINES & INTERSECTION BEHAVIORS

11. The Endless Horizon (Perfectly Horizontal Line)
• The Code: A completely flat line running perfectly parallel to the floor grid.
• The Slope Value: The slope of this line is exactly Zero (\(0\)). There is no rise, only run.
• The Variable Rule: The vertical wall variable (Y) is locked completely dead; it cannot change no matter how much the floor variable (X) grows.
• Cengage Meaning: Represents a market with Infinite Elasticity (buyers are perfectly sensitive to price changes) (This is Cengage info).
12. The Brick Wall (Perfectly Vertical Line)
• The Code: A completely upright line running straight up to the sky, parallel to the side wall.
• The Slope Value: The slope of this line is Undefined (or Infinite). There is infinite rise, but zero run.
• The Variable Rule: The horizontal floor variable (X) is locked completely dead; it stays at one exact number no matter how high or low the wall variable (Y) climbs.
• Cengage Meaning: Represents a market with Perfect Inelasticity (consumers will buy the exact same quantity regardless of price spikes) (This is Cengage info).
13. The Yield Sign (The Tangent Point)
• The Code: A single coordinates address where a straight helper line kisses the outside edge of a sweeping curve without cutting through it.
• The Concept: This point tells you the exact slope of a bending curve at one precise, individual moment in time.
• Where to look: Look for the exact touchpoint where a straight line and a curved line share the identical address grid square.
14. The Mountain Peak (The Maximum Point)
• The Code: The absolute highest vertex or crown of a curve that bends like an upside-down bowl.
• The Slope Rule: At the exact peak of this mountain, the slope drops to exactly Zero for one split second before heading downhill.
• The Variable Story: This shows you the absolute limit where your vertical variable cannot climb any higher.
• Cengage Meaning: Often represents the point of Maximum Total Revenue for a business (This is Cengage info).
15. The Valley Floor (The Minimum Point)
• The Code: The absolute lowest ditch or baseline of a curve shaped like a regular right-side-up bowl or a smile.
• The Slope Rule: At the very bottom of the valley, the slope hits exactly Zero for one moment before heading back uphill.
• Cengage Meaning: This represents the point of Peak Production Efficiency where per-unit factory costs are at their absolute lowest point (This is Cengage info).
16. The Parallel Flight (Constant Distance Lines)
• The Code: Two distinct lines on the grid that run in the exact same direction and never touch or cross.
• The Slope Rule: Their slopes are exactly identical. If Line 1 has a slope of \(2\), Line 2 has a slope of \(2\).
• Cengage Meaning: This is what it looks like when a curve Shifts perfectly to a new position without changing its shape or sensitivity (This is Cengage info).
17. The Perpendicular Crash (The 90-Degree Cross)
• The Code: Two lines that cross each other at a perfect, sharp square corner intersection.
• The Slope Rule: Their slopes are the negative reciprocals of each other (if one line goes up by \(2\), the crashing line must go down by \(\frac{1}{2}\)).
• The Concept: This visually displays two forces that are operating in complete, total defiance or opposition to one another.
18. The Zero-Out Intercept (Horizontal Intercept)
• The Code: The exact coordinate address where a curve smashes flat into the floor grid line.
• The Variable State: At this exact spot, your vertical wall variable (Price or Y) has dropped down to exactly Zero.
• Cengage Story: This shows you the maximum amount of product people would take if the store gave it away completely for free (This is Cengage info).
19. The Choke Price Intercept (Vertical Intercept)
• The Code: The exact coordinate address where a curve smashes straight into the left-hand wall grid line.
• The Variable State: At this exact spot, your horizontal floor variable (Quantity or X) has shrunk to exactly Zero.
• Cengage Story: This shows the exact "choke price" boundary—the point where an item becomes so expensive that buying stops entirely (This is Cengage info).
20. The Area Matrix (Shaded Rectangles)
• The Code: A multi-sided block formed by connecting an intersection point to both axes using dotted helper lines.
• The Math Calculation: Calculated by multiplying the Height value on the wall by the Width value on the floor.
• The Concept: It translates flat lines on a grid into a solid, two-dimensional box of value.
• Cengage Meaning: This box calculates the raw total of cash changing hands (Total Revenue = Price \(\times \) Quantity) (This is Cengage info).
